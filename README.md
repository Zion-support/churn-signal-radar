## What it does

Churn Signal Radar fuses product usage trends, support sentiment, billing signals, and engagement drops into an early-warning score per account — weeks before the renewal conversation goes cold.

## Key features

- **Multi-signal scoring** — usage, sentiment, tickets, billing in one score.
- **Leading-indicator alerts** — login drops, champion exits, feature abandonment.
- **Save playbooks** — recommended intervention per risk pattern.
- **Renewal timeline view** — risk overlaid on renewal dates.
- **Save-rate analytics** — which plays actually retain accounts.

## Who it's for

Customer success and account teams protecting recurring revenue.
